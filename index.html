Nigeria's Food Crisis Is Solvable

🌾

Knowledge Gap

Over 70% of Nigeria's food is produced by smallholder farmers still using traditional methods with no access to modern agricultural science.

💰

Finance Barrier

Most farmers cannot access formal credit. Without capital, they cannot scale, invest in inputs, or survive a bad season.

🚧

Infrastructure Deficit

Poor roads, storage, and market linkages mean post-harvest losses cost Nigerian farmers billions every year.
